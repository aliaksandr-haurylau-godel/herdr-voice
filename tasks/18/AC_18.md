# AC_18

## as-is

`cancel` is declared in the manifest (`herdr-plugin.toml:44`, title "Dictation:
cancel") and is accepted by the client. In `answer` it is a constant
(`src/daemon.rs:173`):

```rust
"cancel" => (
    Reply::Ok("nothing to cancel".to_string()),
    Control::Continue,
),
```

It never reads or touches the recorder. `needs_target_pane` already excludes it
(`src/daemon.rs:41`), and the comment above that function says what it is meant
to do: "it stops whatever is running and clears what a dead run left behind".

The recorder thread (`src/capture.rs:176-243`) owns the one piece of recorder
state: `running: Option<Running>`. (`remembered: Option<String>` sits beside it
but nothing ever sets it to a value; a take that dies with its device stays in
`running` with the sink's failure recorded, and the next `Start` or `Stop`
reports it.) It accepts two commands, `Start` and `Stop`. `dictate`
reads `running` through `Recorder::start` (an answer of `AlreadyRunning` is the
toggle's second half) and then calls `Recorder::stop`, which transcribes and
delivers the take. `discard` (`src/capture.rs:399`) removes a take's file. The file is written only
when a take is stopped (`src/capture.rs:386`); while a take runs its samples are
in memory.

The hold mechanism (`ptt`) keeps its own state, `HoldState`, in `Runtime`. While a
hold is `Opening`, `Live` or `Ending`, `dictate` refuses with "holding for
<pane>: ..." (`src/daemon.rs:270-290`). A comment there reserves ending a hold at
once for issue #55.

Observed result, from the issue: with a take running, `cancel` answers
`nothing to cancel` with exit 0; the next `dictate` stops that same take and
delivers it.

## to-be

`cancel` reads the same recorder state `dictate` reads.

- A take started by `dictate` is running: `cancel` stops the capture, deletes the
  take's audio file, returns the indicator to idle, and answers with a message
  that says a take was discarded and for which pane. Nothing is transcribed or
  delivered. The next `dictate` starts a new take.
- Nothing is recording: `cancel` answers `nothing to cancel`, as today, and this
  path has its own test.
- A hold is in progress (`Opening`, `Live` or `Ending`): `cancel` does not touch
  it and answers with an error that says a key is being held and the recording
  ends when the key comes up. Ending a hold at once is #55.
- The running take's device has already died: `cancel` still discards the take
  and says so, so the next `dictate` starts a new take instead of reporting the
  device failure.

## requirements

1. The recorder accepts a third command, `Cancel`, handled on the recorder
   thread against the same `running` value `Start` and `Stop` use. No second copy of the state exists.
2. `Cancel` with a running take stops the source, drops the take's samples,
   removes the take's file if one exists, and returns the pane the take was for.
   It never converts, measures or writes audio, so no file is ever created by a
   cancel.
3. `Cancel` with a running take whose device has already failed still discards
   it and reports the discard, not the device failure.
4. `Cancel` with nothing running returns a distinct
   result that the daemon turns into `nothing to cancel`.
5. `Cancel` does not touch `remembered`: the field is not set anywhere today and
   changing that is not part of this issue.
6. After a cancel that discarded a take, the activity published for the tab is
   `Idle`, so the decorated tab label is restored. A cancel that found nothing
   running publishes nothing: a take may be in the pipeline (`Working`), and
   `cancel` does not claim to stop it.
7. The reply for a discarded take names the pane. The wording is stated in
   `DESIGN_18.md`.
8. Tests need no microphone: they use the fake sources in `src/capture.rs`
   (`tests_support`). They cover: cancel of a running take; cancel with nothing
   running; cancel after a device failure; `dictate` after `cancel` starts a new take and
   does not deliver the cancelled one; cancel refused during a hold; the
   recorder holds no take after a cancel, and no file remains in the takes
   directory.
9. `cancel` still needs no pane and no context (`src/daemon.rs:3093-3140` stay
   true).

## out of scope

- Ending a hold at once (#55).
- Changing the manifest, the client, the wire protocol or the exit-code rules.
- The wording of other replies.
