# AC_30

## as-is

A take is recognised in `transcribe_take` (`src/daemon.rs:894`). The text the
engine returns goes through the rewrite step, is recorded if `[record]` asks for
it, is journalled as `delivering: <text>`, and is delivered to the pane. When
delivery succeeds the reply is `delivered to <pane> [<level> dB]` and, unless
`[record] transcripts` keeps recordings, the take's file is removed
(`src/daemon.rs:976-989`). Nothing between recognition and delivery looks at what
the text says.

The only check on a take that reaches recognition is the level: a take whose mean
level is under `[audio] silence_db` (default -60 dB) is refused before
recognition (`src/capture.rs`, `stop_one`). `docs/evidence.md:445` records why
that cannot be tuned to catch silence: a minute of room tone measured -54.4 dB,
passed the floor, and the transcriber returned "Продолжение следует..." four
times over, while real speech measured as quiet as -50.9 dB. No threshold
separates the two.

No code in `src/` checks a transcript for repetition. The issue (#30) has no
comments.

## to-be

A transcript that consists of one phrase repeated is reported as probably not
speech. The take is kept, its path is named, and the text is delivered as it is
today. Ordinary speech is not reported.

## requirements

1. **What is flagged.** After recognition, before delivery, the text is compared
   with itself: it is split into words, case and surrounding punctuation are
   ignored, and the text is flagged when the whole of it is the same block of one
   or more words repeated at least three times in a row. Nothing else is flagged.
   In particular:
   - the same phrase appearing among other words is not flagged;
   - a block that appears twice is not flagged;
   - a block repeated three times with any other word before, between or after is
     not flagged;
   - an empty or whitespace-only transcript is not flagged.
2. **What happens to a flagged take.**
   - The text is delivered exactly as it would have been.
   - The take's file is not removed after delivery, whether or not `[record]
     transcripts` is set.
   - The person is told, in the reply of `dictate`, in the journal, and, when
     `[ui] toasts` is on, in a toast. The message says that the text is one phrase
     repeated, how many times, that the take is kept and where, and what to
     check. The exact wording is in `DESIGN_30.md` and is sent to the owner before
     implementation.
   - A hold (`ptt`) has no reply to carry the message, so the journal and the
     toast are its report.
3. **What does not change.** `[audio] silence_db` and every other threshold; the
   decision to refuse a take for its level; delivery, which is never blocked or
   delayed; the take being deleted after delivery when it is not flagged.
4. **The reply stays one line.** The warning in the reply contains no newline and
   no text from the transcript (`tasks/19`: a reply is one line, and transcript
   text can contain newlines).
5. **Tests use no microphone and no model.** The recogniser is a fake engine
   returning a given text. They cover: the example from the issue flagged with
   the count 4; ordinary speech that repeats a word not flagged; flagged and
   unflagged texts delivered identically; the file kept when flagged and removed
   when not; the warning present in the reply, in the journal and, with toasts
   on, in a toast, and absent with toasts off for the toast only.

## out of scope

- Trimming silence from a take (#54).
- Refusing, discarding or blocking a take; changing any level threshold.
- Detecting hallucination in general: partial repetition, a repeated phrase
  following real speech, or any other shape.
- The wording of other messages.

## by hand (S5)

A daemon built from this worktree with its own state and configuration, never the
owner's. A transcriber that returns one phrase four times and a transcriber that
returns ordinary text, each driven through two `dictate` presses: show the
warning, the kept file, and the delivered text for the first, and none of those
for the second.
